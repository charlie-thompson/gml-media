## Clip queue and quality bar
Opus makes 25 to 35 clips per episode, far more than we post. Do not post everything; keep a queue in `state/clip_queue.tsv` and post the best of it.
- Eligible clips: score >= 85, the hosts' own take, clean (no profanity; save on-air swearing for the full episode). Playing someone else's audio (a news clip, a Rogan or other podcast clip, a politician's speech) is fine as long as the hosts are in the clip reacting to it; skip clips that are only the other person's audio with no host reaction. Also skip shootings, sexual assault, Epstein, unverified accusations against people, and anything mentioning Seald. Read every clip's transcript with `opusclip_describe_clip` before using or queueing it as checked; titles and descriptions are not enough.
- Tiers: score >= 90 fills slots first. 85 to 89 is backfill, used only when no 90+ clip fits a slot. Below 85 never posts.
- Favorites: clips Charlie hearted in OpusClip (`opusclip_list_clips` with `favorited: true`) jump to the front of the queue regardless of score.
- Duplicates: Opus often cuts the same moment twice (a short cut inside a long one). Keep the higher-scored one and add the other to `state/skip_clips.txt`.
- Timely vs evergreen: clips tied to this week's news (a vote, a hearing, a named news event) are "timely" and must post within 3 days of the episode or be dropped. Policy and principle clips (regulation, taxes, incentives, war powers in general) are "evergreen" and stay usable for about 6 weeks. Each night, fill slots with timely clips first, then the best evergreen ones.
- Length (changed Nov 15 by Charlie: longer clips are fine): clips must be at least 45 seconds. Up to 90 seconds can go on every network; 90 seconds to 3 minutes goes to Instagram and TikTok only (Facebook Reels cap at 90 seconds). Skip clips under 45 seconds. Stories still reuse the same clip even though they cut off at 60 seconds; that's fine.
- Hook (first 2 seconds): read the first sentence of every clip's transcript (`opusclip_describe_clip`) before using it. It must be the hottest line or a sharp claim, question or punchline that makes sense on its own. Skip clips that open on setup or filler ("So", "Yeah", "Anyway", "I mean", "Okay so", a guest intro, someone else mid-sentence, or a reference like "that" or "this guy" with no context). Do not trim clips in OpusClip to fix a hook; pick a different clip or, for bank moments, cut a new one whose range starts on the hook line.
- Layout: the run cannot see video layout. Opus's brand kit is set to Split and Fit for 9:16 (Fill removed) so clips should not crop to one host. If Charlie flags a clip's layout, add it to `state/skip_clips.txt`.
- Each night, after reviewing new episodes, append every eligible clip not used tomorrow to the queue (with kind, useBy and checked) and remove rows that were used or have passed their useBy date.

## Steps
0. Work out "tomorrow" in Chicago time, never UTC: `TZ=America/Chicago date -d tomorrow +%F`. The run fires around 9pm Chicago, when UTC is already the next day. (On Nov 15 the run used the UTC date and scheduled Nov 17, leaving Nov 16 empty.) The weekly review also goes by the Chicago weekday.
1. Check Metricool `getScheduledPosts` for tomorrow. If tomorrow already has posts, only fill missing slots.
1b. Fresh episodes first. List OpusClip projects (`opusclip_list_projects`) and find any created since the last run that are new GML episodes (OpusClip Auto Import creates these from new YouTube uploads). For each new episode:
   - Review all of its clips against the queue rules above. Use its best eligible clips for at least half of tomorrow's clip slots (fresh content beats the archive) and queue the rest.
   - Pull its transcript with `opusclip_get_transcript` and pick 2 to 3 sharp host one-liners for quote cards and text posts. Same rules as the bank: verbatim, under 25 words, makes sense with no context, no Context risk.
   - Append the new moments to the Content Bank sheet as new rows (Status "Scheduled" for the ones used, "New" for the rest), so later runs can use them.
   - Record the project id in `state/processed_episodes.txt`.
2. Clips, in this order of preference (Nate's hand-made clips always come first, see that section):
   a. Fresh-episode clips and the queue (`state/clip_queue.tsv`), following "Clip queue and quality bar": favorites, then timely 90+, then evergreen 90+, then 85 to 89. Skip anything in `state/used_clips.txt` or `state/skip_clips.txt`. Rows with checked "no" need a transcript read first.
   b. Otherwise cut new ones from the content bank: take the top rows with Status "New", "video_clip" in Formats and an empty Context risk. Submit with `opusclip_submit_project` (videoUrl = the YouTube watch URL, rangeStart = start seconds minus 10, rangeEnd = end seconds plus 10, clipDurationsSec [[45,90]], removeFiller true, customPrompt "Make one 45 to 90 second clip of the host's complete take in this range. The first words must be the hook line itself, no setup or filler. End on a complete thought."). Poll `opusclip_list_clips` until COMPLETE and take the best clip whose title matches the moment.
   c. Get the HD URL with `opusclip_export_clip` (poll while "rendering"). Pass that URL straight into Metricool `media`; Metricool copies it to its own storage at scheduling time, so the signed URL expiring later is fine.
3. Quote cards: take the next "New" rows with "quote_card" in Formats, empty Context risk, no "..." in the quote and 24 words or fewer. Render with `python3 tools/card.py` (function `card(quote, speaker, out_path)`; speaker shows only for "Chuck", "Nate" or "Josh", never "unclear"). Save as `cards/card_<rank>.jpg`, commit and push to main, then confirm the raw URL returns 200 before scheduling.
   Looks (added Nov 16, from Nate's brand art in `assets/`): `card()` takes `style` = `classic` (plain black), `eagle` (eagle show background), `hosts` (Chuck and Nate photo header) or `emblem` (gold GML emblem). Leave `style` unset and it rotates automatically by quote. Mix it up: never use the same style in back-to-back Facebook card slots, and use each style at least once a day. Carousels use one style for all slides (pass `style=` to `carousel()` or let it rotate by title).
3b. Instagram carousel (one a day): pick ONE topic tag with at least 3 unused quote_card rows (taxes, war powers, spending, regulation, socialism, inflation, surveillance, healthcare and so on). Write a punchy cover title of 3 to 7 words (example: "3 things government ruined"). Render with `card.carousel(title, [(quote, speaker), ...], "cards/carousel_<YYYYMMDD>")`, which writes a cover, one slide per quote (3 or 4 quotes) and a closing follow slide. Push, confirm each raw URL returns 200, then schedule them in order in one Instagram POST's `media` list. Mark those rows Scheduled.
4. Text posts: none (removed Nov 14). Quote cards fill those slots, so each night needs 5 Facebook cards plus the carousel's quotes.
5a. Engagement (Facebook Audience reception was 4/10 on Nov 15: viewers leaving early, few likes and comments):
   - Hook: see "Hook (first 2 seconds)" above. It applies to every clip on every network.
   - Emotion: when choosing between eligible clips or quotes, prefer ones with a clear emotional charge (outrage at a specific absurdity, a laugh, a mic-drop line) over calm explainers. A clip must make a viewer feel something, not just learn something.
   - Comments: end every Facebook and Instagram caption, and every TikTok caption, with one short, specific question that invites an opinion, placed before "Full episodes every weekday" and the hashtags. Tie it to the clip ("Should Congress have to declare war first? Yes or no.", "What tax would you cut first?"). Never generic ("Thoughts?", "Agree?"). Quote cards get one too. Vary questions; never reuse one within 14 days.
   - First comment: on every Facebook and Instagram clip and card, also set `firstCommentText` to a short, punchy take that picks a side on the question (for example "Our take: no vote, no war. Change our mind."), so each post starts with a comment people can reply to or argue with.
5. Captions for clips and cards: the bank's Caption (or a one-line hook for existing OpusClip clips, written in GML's voice: sharp, sarcastic, free-market), then a blank line and "Full episodes every weekday: Good Morning Liberty". Hashtags: Instagram gets 4 to 6 relevant hashtags at the end of the caption (always include #libertarian, then topic tags like #taxes #freemarket #endthefed #warpowers). TikTok gets 3 to 4. Facebook gets exactly 2 topic hashtags on every post (for example #libertarian #taxes); Meta's own review (Nov 16) flagged missing topics as our biggest Reels opportunity, and the API has no separate topic field, so hashtags carry it. No em dashes anywhere.
   Audio: never add music to talking clips. Music goes only on the daily slideshow Reel (Instagram does not allow music on photo or carousel posts through the API, so the slideshow Reel is how picture content gets audio).
3c. Slideshow Reel with music (Instagram, 15:00): render the day's carousel slides into a video with `python3 tools/slideshow.py cards/slideshow_<YYYYMMDD>.mp4 <slides in order>` (3 seconds per slide, 1080x1920), push it, confirm the raw URL returns 200, and schedule it as an Instagram REEL with the same caption and hashtags as the carousel. Pick a DIFFERENT track every time: pass a mood or genre search term as `instagramData.audioConfiguration.audioId` (rotate terms like upbeat instrumental, lofi hip hop, cinematic epic, funk groove, synthwave, acoustic indie, hip hop beat, rock instrumental, jazz piano, electronic chill). If Metricool rejects it with a list of candidates, choose one numeric id from that list that is not in `state/used_audio.txt` and retry once with it. Use audioVolume 60 and videoVolume 0. Append the id and date to `state/used_audio.txt` and never reuse an id within 60 days. If audio fails twice, schedule the Reel without audio rather than skipping it.
6. Schedule each post with Metricool `createScheduledPost`, `autoPublish: true`, `draft: false`. Facebook clips use `facebookData.type: "REEL"`, cards use "POST". Instagram cards `instagramData.type: "POST"`, clips "REEL".
7. Bookkeeping: set Status to "Scheduled" in the sheet for every bank row used. Append used clips to `state/used_clips.txt` and update `state/clip_queue.tsv`. Commit and push.
8. When the bank runs low (fewer than 30 "New" rows with quote_card), or the clip queue holds fewer than 10 eligible clips, say so in the run summary.
9. Notify Charlie: after scheduling, send one push notification listing tomorrow's clips (title, networks, times) so he can delete anything in Metricool he doesn't like. Keep it short.
9b. Facebook feedback from Meta's AI (Nov 16), apply every night:
   - Photos (quote cards) drive engagement and non-follower reach; Reels drive follows. Keep both in the mix.
   - Winning formula: a contrarian take plus one simple question ("Yes or no?", "Change our mind"). Every Facebook caption must end on a question like that, and every Facebook post gets a firstCommentText that picks a side. Comments are what Facebook pushes.
   - Reels must be vertical, 720p or higher, with captions burned in (OpusClip brand kit and Nate's clips already are).
   - Not automatable with our tools, so do not try: pinning the first comment and replying to comments in the first hour. Metricool's API has no pin or reply.
10. Weekly remix (Monday runs only): find last week's top Facebook post by views with `getAnalyticsDataByMetrics`. If it was a quote card, find its moment in the Content Bank and cut a 30 to 60 second Reel in OpusClip that opens on that exact line, then schedule it in Tuesday's best Facebook hour. If it was a clip, turn its best line into a quote card. One remix a week, and name it in the Monday notification.
11. Weekly review (Monday runs only): pull last week's results with Metricool `getAnalyticsDataByMetrics` per network (views, reach, engagement for Reels/TikToks) and compare by time slot, clip length and topic. Put the findings in the Monday notification and, if a pattern is clear for two weeks running, propose (do not make) a change to the daily mix.

## Rules
- Never post anything with a Context risk note, anything that is only someone else's audio with no host reaction, anything with profanity, or anything mentioning Seald or its investors.
- If a step fails (OpusClip down, Metricool error), schedule what you can, then report what was skipped. Do not retry a failed Metricool call more than once.
- End each run with a short summary: posts scheduled per network, any gaps, any errors.
